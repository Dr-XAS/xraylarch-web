# Week of October 5, 2026

Author: Jeffrey Huang (jhuang165)

## What I did

- Added **Simulate EXAFS from CIF** to the attached-structure/FEFF workflow,
  including empty projects. Shared S₀², σ², ΔE₀ and ΔR controls sum all available
  FEFF paths (up to 100) or the chosen subset without fitting measured data.
  Results include χ(k), |χ(R)|, raw-χ CSV, complex-R CSV and a simulation JSON
  containing the exact CIF, FEFF input, path files and parameters. Assumptions,
  selected site, omitted-path warnings and uncorrected R distances stay explicit.

- Added right-click copying from **Fit range & transform** to all marked groups,
  with individual-field actions, a **⋯** menu, and keyboard access. Copies preserve
  each group's FEFF paths, fit parameters, unfinished edits, and saved history,
  validate every resulting range first, and use the existing autosave queue.
- Added **Upload CIF** in Crystal structures and its search dialog. Custom
  structures retain the original CIF text, filename, and checksum in the project,
  support visualization and FEFF, and survive JSON/PRJ exchange and Undo/Redo.
- Validate single-structure CIFs up to 500 KB, deduplicate by exact content,
  preserve distinct files with identical names, and retain warnings for readable
  structures that cannot be used for FEFF.
