For the 10 K Cu foil, I normalized μ(E), removed the AUTOBK background, and fit the Cu–Cu first shell over **k = 3–10 Å⁻¹** and **R = 1–3 Å**. The fit varied \(S_0^2\), \(\Delta E_0\), \(\sigma^2\), and \(\Delta R\), then repeated the fit across five nearby k/R windows; it was judged **robust**.

- **Nearest-neighbour Cu–Cu distance:** **2.552 Å**
- **Practical reportable value:** **2.55 Å**
- **Formal fit uncertainty:** about **±0.001 Å**
- **Window-sensitivity spread:** about **0.003 Å** overall

So you can trust the result comfortably to **a few hundredths of an ångström**—reporting **2.55 Å** is justified. I would not claim precision much better than roughly **±0.01 Å** without independently addressing absolute energy calibration and model/systematic errors.
