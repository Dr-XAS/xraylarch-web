The 10 K Cu foil was normalized and AUTOBK-background-subtracted, then fitted with the Cu–Cu first-shell FEFF path over **k = 3–10 Å⁻¹** and **R = 1–3 Å**. **S0², ΔE0, σ², and ΔR were all varied**, and the fit was repeated over several alternative k/R windows.

- **Nearest-neighbour Cu–Cu distance: 2.552 Å**
- To the requested precision: **2.55 Å**
- Fit uncertainty: about **±0.001 Å**
- Window-sweep variation: about **0.003 Å**
- Practical trust: **the hundredths-of-an-angstrom digit is well supported; report 2.55 Å rather than claiming precision finer than roughly 0.01 Å.**

The result is rated **robust** across the tested fitting windows. The main limitation is the strong expected correlation between \(S_0^2\) and \(\sigma^2\), but that correlation has little effect on the fitted bond distance here.
