The **10 K and 50 K copper foil scans are comparable**:

- Nearly identical E0: difference **0.00 eV**
- Edge-step ratio: **0.996**
- XANES maximum difference: only **0.0086**
- Very small alignment shift: **−0.018 eV**
- Similar EXAFS amplitude, with the expected gradual weakening at higher \(k\) from temperature-related damping
- Both cover essentially the same range and have \(k_\text{max}=25\ \text{Å}^{-1}\)

The **300 K scan is not comparable as currently loaded**. It differs substantially:

- E0 is shifted by **+2.92 eV**
- Alignment would require a large **−2.959 eV** shift
- Edge step is **18.7% larger**
- XANES difference is **0.3842**, far beyond the 10/50 K difference
- Its usable energy range ends at **10,145.86 eV**, versus about **11,362 eV** for the other scans
- Its available \(k\) range is only **17.45 Å⁻¹**, compared with **25 Å⁻¹**
- EXAFS amplitude falls dramatically relative to the 10 K scan, reaching only **8.4%** in the 15–17.45 Å⁻¹ region
- It is byte-for-byte identical to the separate **“Cu foil · shared reference”** group, so it appears to be a duplicated reference measurement rather than an independent 300 K scan

So, before processing: **treat the 10 K and 50 K scans as the comparable temperature series; exclude the 300 K group unless you can verify the source file and correct its identity/range.**
